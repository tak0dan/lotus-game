LOTUS

A tabletop strategy game of sacrifice, faith and territorial dominion

Aurelia was a prosperous realm until the Great Cathedral sank. Now its towers loom over exhausted land, every institution promises that the suffering has a purpose — and the Lotuses, older than the kingdoms, wait to be called back at a price.

“In a world where everything demands a sacrifice, what is the difference between faith and exploitation?”

The short version

  • Roll a D6. Move that many units one action each, or summon the unit on the die (1 Archer · 2 Infantry · 3 Cavalry · 4 Ship · 6 Tower) beside a Tower. 5 is the Ritual: sacrifice a unit for rerolls, hoping to call a Lotus.
  • Chess-like: pieces move in fixed patterns and a capture removes the piece. Infantry creep as formations; Cavalry charge; Archers shoot.
  • Win by holding the gold control points with Towers, or by leaving the enemy with no Towers and no Infantry. The Codex button has every rule.
