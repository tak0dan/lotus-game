# Jihui — sources
**There is no published rulebook.** Everything below is a scene, a wiki summary of scenes, or a quest line.

## The novel (primary)
- *World of Warcraft: Vol'jin — Shadows of the Horde*, Michael A. Stackpole (Gallery Books, 2013). Read from the designer's own PDF copy in `~/Books/` (169 pages; not copied here).
- Scenes used (PDF pages): **27–29** (the game taught), 37, 39, 44–45, 73–74, 87, 116, 118, **127–129** (the fireship); the **philosophy** (Tushui/Huojin/balance) runs through 19–20, 24–25, 30–31, 35, 60, 62, 74, 87, 90, 94, 104, 124, 132. Found by a vocabulary search (balance, cube, pieces, canister, …) over every page, then each hit read in context. A scene that discusses the game *without* those words could still exist — if you know one, give me the page.
- Also on the Internet Archive (borrowable scans, full-text indexed): `voljinshadowsofh0000stac` (308 pp.) and `pdfy-1IFBRyNqeLOvYPjk`.

## How I searched the Internet Archive
Full-text search (the `fts` backend of archive.org's search API) for "jihui", "jihui pandaria", "jihui Wrathion", "jihui Tushui Huojin". "jihui" returns **3,159** hits, almost all noise (Chinese pinyin *jihui* = "opportunity", personal names). The Warcraft-relevant hits are only the two *Vol'jin* scans and *War Crimes*. **No other book on the Archive appears to contain the game.** The search returns snippets, not full text, for borrowable books.
