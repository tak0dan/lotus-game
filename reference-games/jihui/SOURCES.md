# Jihui — sources
- *World of Warcraft: Vol'jin — Shadows of the Horde*, Michael A. Stackpole (Gallery Books, 2013). Read from the PDF in `~/Books/` (the designer's own copy, not copied here).
- Main scene: **PDF p.27** (Tyrathan teaches Vol'jin). Other mentions: pp.29, 37, 39, 44, 73–74, 87, 116, 127–128.
- Jihui appears only in the novel; **there is no published rulebook**. The rules in `RULES.md` are reconstructed from that scene and are incomplete (no stated move distances, face tables, capture rules or full piece list).
- The earlier name "Jol'Jihn" = the book's hero **Vol'jin** + the game **jihui**.
