# Warhammer 40,000 — 11th Edition (launched June 2026) — rules summary

> **Read this first.** Written in my own words from *secondary* sources (reviews and explainers of the official free Core Rules), **not from the Core Rules PDF itself** — I could not retrieve it (see `SOURCES.md`). Items marked **(long-standing)** are how the game has worked since 8th edition and are very likely unchanged, but **the pages I could read did not confirm them for 11th**. Check the official PDF before relying on any number.

## What the game is
Two (or more) armies of painted miniatures fight on a tabletop over several **battle rounds**. Distances are in **inches**, outcomes use **D6 dice**, armies are built to a **points** budget. It is the opposite of a deterministic game: dice decide hits, wounds and saves.

## Army building (11th)
Armies are made of **detachments**; a list can now combine several. You get **Detachment Points (DP)**: **2** in a 1,000-point game, **3** in a 2,000-point game; each detachment costs 1–3 DP. 10th-edition codexes remain usable at launch.

## A unit (long-standing)
A unit is one or more models that act together. Typical profile: **Move, Toughness, Save, Wounds, Leadership, Objective Control (OC)**; weapons: **Range, Attacks, Skill (BS shooting / WS melee), Strength, AP, Damage**. Keywords (Infantry, Monster, Vehicle, Fly, Hover, Swarm, Beast, Hero, Towering…) trigger rules.

## The turn — players alternate turns; each turn has phases
1. **Command phase** — start-of-phase abilities resolve; **both players gain 1 Command Point (CP)**; units that are **Battle-shocked** test to recover (a unit stays shocked until it passes, even above half strength); then both check scored objectives.
2. **Movement phase** — options: **Remain Stationary, Normal Move, Advance** (+D6 but no shooting unless Assault), **Fall Back** (battle-shocked units take Hazard rolls; a 1 or 2 is a mortal wound). Models can move through friendly models; **pivoting is gone** (rotate free). **Engagement range is 2″ (5″ vertically).** Reserves arrive by **Ingress Move**, more than **8″** from enemies. Flyers can cross models/terrain at −2″; **Aircraft** no longer move normally (Ingress within 6″ of an edge, can only reach the enemy zone on turns 4–5). Heavy weapons get +1 to hit if the unit moved ≤3″.
3. **Shooting phase** — hit/BS modifiers stack (a +2 to hit is possible). **Cover now gives the attacker −1 BS** (it no longer improves the save). **Higher elevation gives +1 BS** ("plunging fire"). **Hidden/Concealment:** Infantry/Beast/Swarm in dense area terrain that did not shoot (this or last turn) can only be targeted from within **15″**. Indirect fire without a spotter hits only on 6s. Ground floors of buildings block sight; upper floors see out of windows.
4. **Charge phase** — **roll your maximum charge distance first, then pick a target** within that range (you may decline); you only need to end within the 2″ engagement range, not base-to-base. (Like Age of Sigmar.)
5. **Fight phase** — **pile-in for all units at the start** (active player first); players alternate activating units, **Fights First** units go first; the charger chooses the first fight. After fighting, **consolidate** (active player first): units within 3″ of an enemy must move into it, others move toward the nearest objective. An **Overrun Fight** (3″ pile-in) is possible if the charge target dies before the charger activates.

## Resolving an attack — hit → wound → save → damage (long-standing)
Roll to **hit** (BS/WS), then to **wound** (Strength vs the target's Toughness), the defender rolls a **save** (Save modified by the weapon's AP), unsaved wounds apply **Damage** to a model. What 11th changed, per the sources: the defender **lines up models and rolls all saves at once**; **hits are allocated to specific models/groups** chosen by the saving player (lowest dice first; **bodyguards die before Leaders**, Characters last); multi-unit targets use the majority Toughness; Precision spills onto bodyguards more easily. Psychic attacks ignore hit/BS/WS modifiers. **Blast** now scales (Blast 3 = +3 attacks per 5 models); **Cleave** is the melee version; **Hazardous** 1–2 = mortal wound.

## Unit coherency (the "battalion" rule) — simplified in 11th
Each model must be within **2″ of at least one other model of its unit**, and within **9″ of all the others**. (Sources differ slightly on the exact wording — check the PDF.) A unit that breaks coherency has to fix it.

## Objectives and scoring
**Round markers are gone.** Objectives are **specific pieces of terrain** (buildings, crates, downed landers); a unit claims one by being **inside** it, and can contest from ruins. **Objective Secured** is now a core rule. Each player gets a **primary mission from their Force Disposition** relative to the opponent's: *Take and Hold, Purge the Foe, Disruption, Reconnaissance, Priority Assets.* (How OC is compared and how points are awarded was **not** in the pages I could read.)

## Command Points and stratagems
Both players gain **1 CP per Command phase**. **A unit can be affected by only one stratagem per phase.** **Fire Overwatch** — at the end of the enemy's Movement phase, a unit may shoot. **Heroic Intervention** — normally only into units that charged; for an extra CP into any enemy within 6″. **Crushing Impact** (monsters deal damage on a charge) replaces Tank Shock.

## Leaders
Attached units are now a **Leader** and a **Support** unit; keywords are shared; **Leader abilities persist when the Bodyguard dies.** Transports: models may disembark straight into combat when their transport is engaged.

## What the project looks to it for
Unit **coherency** (formations that must stay together, the Infantry-as-battalion idea) · **phases** inside a turn · **objectives held by presence** · **limits on stacking buffs** (one stratagem per unit per phase) · **overwatch** (reaction) · **battle-shock** (a morale state that disables a unit). Everything dice-based (hit/wound/save) is the opposite of the Tower Game's deterministic ATK-vs-DEF and would be a conscious departure.
