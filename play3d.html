How to play. Each year you plan orders, then press End year; the AI plans in secret and everything resolves together: economy → recruitment → movement → combat → building → control → upkeep. Select a unit: blue squares are where it can move (click to order), red rings are enemies it can strike (click to attack). Siege engines are the only way to hurt towers and must not move. Select a tower to recruit or add levels; select an Infantry to lay a foundation. Win: hold the enemy fortress through a full year, hold a majority of the gold-ringed points (garrisoned towers) for 2 years, or leave the enemy without towers and builders.
