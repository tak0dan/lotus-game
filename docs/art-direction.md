# Art direction (designer's feedback; applies to every generation)

Source of truth = the designer's own reference images (`assets/More/`: Iron Fist, Iron Fist Shielded, Heavy Iron Fist; the War Priest series). Every generated image is checked against them before it is kept. One generation is never enough: generate several, compare, send the specific problems back, regenerate.

## Feedback log
- **Runic gauntlet Monk, Grok edit #1 (2026-11-10):** designer verdict: "This is a terrible design. Weapons are way too oversized, they have no style, too much fantasy bullshit, they don't match the character style." Follow-up in progress: "They don't have to be oversized, they have to be ..." (designer was typing).
  - Rules derived: gauntlets stay **plausible armour**, same scale and finish as the reference gauntlets; the fire runes are small, restrained, engraved (not glowing slabs); no cartoon proportions; match the reference's sober, dark, painted, realistic look; no extra fantasy ornament.
