# ChatGPT consult 2 — Monk lore (follow-up conversation)

Context: after the first lore pass, the designer told ChatGPT (temporary chat) that Monks are heavy bare-handed melee fighters in plate gloves, philosophically between the Air Nomads (Avatar) and the Witcher school warriors, that they gather spiritual resources instinctively through trance-like concentration (and that nobody, not even other spiritual practitioners, can explain their efficiency), and that **Monks have no heavy version**: they wear chainmail under leather school jackets for speed; the "heavier" version is a branch that uses **enchanted gauntlets with fire runes that explode on contact with armour — no armour saves from them, only a Priest's prayers**. The designer also pasted a private TRPG character/lore document as inspiration (not reproduced here). Below is what ChatGPT answered, transcribed from the page.

## Monk — Revised Codex Entry (ChatGPT)
The Monks belong to ancient martial orders whose origins predate the Fall of Aurelia. Organised into independent schools, they preserve generations of martial teachings, alchemical knowledge and physical disciplines developed to push the mortal body beyond its ordinary limitations. Each school follows its own combat doctrine, specialising in different techniques and equipment, though all share the same fundamental principles: discipline, control and the relentless pursuit of practical mastery.

Those serving on the battlefields of Aurelia wear interlinked chainmail beneath reinforced leather school jackets, providing protection without sacrificing the mobility required by their fighting style. Their hands are covered by specialised iron gauntlets, designed to deliver devastating blows against armoured opponents. They carry no conventional weapons, having spent years conditioning their bodies to become weapons in their own right. Their combat doctrine favours decisive engagement: they close the distance, deliver their strikes and withdraw precisely as far as they advanced, leaving their opponents little opportunity to retaliate.

Beyond physical training, the Monks preserve fragments of ancient alchemical knowledge and possess an unusual relationship with spiritual resources. Through intense concentration, sometimes entering a trance-like state, they instinctively draw raw spiritual energy into their bodies and give it form. Unlike conventional practitioners, they require neither elaborate rituals nor extensive theoretical understanding. Even scholars who have dedicated their lives to studying Soul and other spiritual resources cannot explain the extraordinary efficiency with which Monks manipulate them. The Monks themselves have little interest in explaining what they accomplish. To them, it is simply another discipline to be perfected.

## Heavy Gauntlets — The Runic Discipline (ChatGPT)
The most destructive gauntlets are inscribed with fire runes, carefully engraved and bound to enchanted stones. Upon contact with an opponent's armour, the runes release their stored energy in a violent internal explosion, turning protective plate into a death sentence for the wearer. Conventional armour offers no meaningful defence; the force is delivered through the point of impact, destroying the body beneath the metal rather than attempting to penetrate it. Only the protective wards invoked by Priests can withstand the resulting devastation.

These weapons are not issued indiscriminately. Their construction demands specialised knowledge, rare materials and an understanding of runic interactions that few surviving craftsmen possess. To wield them effectively requires the same discipline expected of every Monk: the power of the weapon must never exceed the control of its bearer.
